Airport Parking Information

Lviv Danylo Halytskyi International Airport (LWO)
- Official airport parking is available directly in front of the terminal.
- Nearby streets where parking may be available:
  - Liubinska Street
  - Riashivska Street
  - Aviatsiina Street
- Long-term parking is recommended at the official airport parking area.

Boryspil International Airport (KBP), Kyiv
- Official long-term and short-term parking lots are available at the airport terminals.
- Nearby roads and streets:
  - Boryspil Highway (M03)
  - Kyivskyi Shliakh Street
  - Terminal D Access Road
- Visitors should use designated airport parking whenever possible.

Kyiv International Airport (Zhuliany) (IEV)
- Official parking is located next to Terminal A.
- Nearby streets:
  - Medova Street
  - Povitroflotskyi Avenue
  - Volynska Street
- Short-term parking is available close to the terminal entrance.

Warsaw Chopin Airport (WAW)
- Official airport parking includes P1, P2, P4, Premium, and Kiss & Fly parking areas.
- Nearby streets:
  - Żwirki i Wigury Street
  - Wirażowa Street
  - Szyszkowa Street
  - Astronautów Street
- Several private parking facilities with shuttle service are located on Szyszkowa Street. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

John Paul II Kraków Airport (KRK)
- Official airport parking is available near the passenger terminal.
- Nearby streets:
  - Kapitana Mieczysława Medweckiego Street
  - Balicka Street
  - Na Lotnisko Street

Budapest Ferenc Liszt International Airport (BUD)
- Official airport parking is available for short-term and long-term stays.
- Nearby streets:
  - Vecsési Road
  - Ferihegyi Repülőtér Road
  - Üllői Road
